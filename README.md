# MineKart
Minecraft meets Mario Kart in this multiplayer mash-up.

## Installation
1. Download the [mcaddon](https://github.com/kirbycope/MineKart/releases/latest/download/MineKart.mcaddon) from the latest release
1. Double-click the mcaddon file
1. Load the Resource and Behaviour packs for your world/save

## Behaviors
 - ![Block](/item-box.gif) Item Box: Gives the player a random item.
 - ![Entity](/development_resource_packs/MineKart/textures/items/mario_kart_spawn_egg_32_50.png) 50cc Mario Kart: Spawns a kart with 6 HP.
 - ![Entity](/development_resource_packs/MineKart/textures/items/mario_kart_spawn_egg_32_100.png) 100cc Mario Kart: Spawns a kart with 6 HP.
 - ![Entity](/development_resource_packs/MineKart/textures/items/mario_kart_spawn_egg_32_150.png) 150cc Mario Kart: Spawns a kart with 6 HP.
 - Hauler and Dart: The same three speed classes as the Mario Kart, in a wide, tall Hauler body and a long, low Dart body.
 - Dye: Use a dye while riding a Mario Kart to repaint it red, orange, yellow, green, purple, black or white.
 - ![Item](/development_resource_packs/MineKart/textures/items/banana_32.png) Banana: Drops a peel behind you. The next kart to drive over it spins out and nearly stops for 2 seconds.
 - ![Item](/development_resource_packs/MineKart/textures/items/blooper_32.png) Blooper: Gives all other players blindness for 6 seconds.
 - ![Item](/development_resource_packs/MineKart/textures/items/bob-omb_32.png) Bob-omb: Explodes where it lands, dealing 6 damage.
 - ![Item](/development_resource_packs/MineKart/textures/items/boo_32.png) Boo: Gives the player invisibility/invulnerability for 10 seconds.
 - ![Item](/development_resource_packs/MineKart/textures/items/green_shell_32.png) Green Shell: Throws a shell straight ahead, dealing 6 damage to whatever it hits.
 - ![Item](/development_resource_packs/MineKart/textures/items/lightning_32.png) Lightning: Hits every other kart, of any class or color, with a lightning bolt.
 - ![Item](/development_resource_packs/MineKart/textures/items/mushroom_32.png) Mushroom: Gives your kart a 4 second speed boost.
 - ![Item](/development_resource_packs/MineKart/textures/items/red_shell_32.png) Red Shell: Homes in on the nearest kart ahead of it (within 48 blocks, never your own), dealing 6 damage.
 - ![Item](/development_resource_packs/MineKart/textures/items/spiny_shell_32.png) Spiny Shell: Chases the nearest other kart in any direction (within 128 blocks), dealing 6 damage.
 - ![Item](/development_resource_packs/MineKart/textures/items/star_32.png) Star: Gives the player some temporary buffs.

## Track pieces
Each of these acts on the kart, not the player, so they only matter while you drive; walking around is never slowed.
 - Boost Pad: A kart driving over it gets Speed III for 3 seconds.
 - Oil Puddle: A kart driving through it gets Slowness III for 4 seconds.
 - Off-road: A kart on grass, dirt, sand, gravel, mud and the like gets Slowness I while it stays there. Stay on the track!

Use an item (right-click, or tap on touch) to fire it. Shells and Bob-ombs fly where you aim.
